# Ex Ilva in Taranto: research for the site (18/11/2026)

Legend: **[P]** read on a primary/official source · **[S]** press or secondary sources only · **TO VERIFY** open doubt.
The Barbascura eXtra video is used only as a guide (`sources/video-barbascura-notes.md`), never as a source.

## In brief

- **Existing projects:** no interactive map, StoryMap or scrollytelling on Ilva. Only text timelines exist (Il Post 2026, Euronews 2025). The space is open.
- **The "chiuso nel 2008" [closed in 2008]:**
  - No main plant closes. 2008 brings the **dioxin in pecorino** (Feb–Mar), the **culling of the flocks** of the farmsteads and the **regional anti-dioxin law** (Dec).
  - In the video a former worker says that "area a freddo, tubifici e rivestimenti sono chiusi dal 2008" [cold area, pipe mills and coatings have been closed since 2008]. The shutdowns of pipe mills and pipe coating due to the market crisis are documented. The 2008 date is **TO VERIFY**.
- **Mortality:** only estimates by period or scenario exist, never an annual series. Deaths at work are press counts, not official ones.
- **Pollution:** hourly ARPA data from 2005, emissions declared by the plant from 2007, CO2 from 2005. Before 2005 almost nothing was measured. The gap must be shown, not filled.
- **Map:**
  - OSM has the detailed geometry of the plant (relation 11843712, about 15 km²). The department names are missing, though, and must be added by hand.
  - Historical orthophotos exist (1988, 1994, 2000, 2006, 2012 national; 1997–2023 regional), but with an uncertain licence: they can be shown via WMS, not re-hosted.
  - The 1954 GAI flight (the "before") cannot be downloaded for free.

## 2. Health and mortality

**Rule for the site:** always keep **counts** (deaths at work, observed cases) separate from **attributable estimates** (models). Every number must always come with period, population, source and confidence interval.

### Estimates of deaths attributable to emissions

| Estimate | Period | Population | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2012 expert report, short-term effect (PM10 above the WHO limit of 20 µg/m³) | 2004–2010 | Borgo + Tamburi | **91 deaths** (80% CI 55–127); about **40** after correcting for frailty. Also 160 cardiac and 219 respiratory hospitalisations | [P] Camera Doc. CCIV-bis n.1 leg. XVII, p.113 |
| 2012 expert report, long-term effect | 1998–2010 (13 years) | cohort Taranto, Massafra, Statte | **386 deaths** (30/year, 1.4% of the total); 237 cancers, 937 respiratory hospitalisations (638 paediatric) | [P] text quoted via ISDE/PeaceLink and Domani; original report not opened |
| WHO, impact assessment | 10 years before the AIA (2010 scenario) | adults >30 years | **270–430** premature deaths; 5–8/year in the post-AIA 2015 scenario | [S] Quotidiano Sanità 21/01/2022 |
| VIIAS 2019 | scenarios 2010 / 2012 / 2015 | Taranto | about **28 / 18 / 5** deaths/year | [S] Quotidiano Sanità 24/10/2019 |

### Editorial choice (18/11/2026)
**Model estimates are not shown on the site**: the 386 and 91 deaths of the expert report, the 270 of the WHO assessment, the 22,500 residents of the VDS. They are too easy to dispute until we cite them from the original documents. They remain in `events.js` with `kind: 'estimate'` and are filtered out when the page starts, so they can be re-enabled by removing one line.

Only **counts and measurements** remain on the site: the 208 deaths at work, the +54% of childhood cancers, benzo(a)pyrene and mesothelioma ×3.7.

### What can be shown graphically
- **Bars by period:** SENTIERI (with the caveat that methods change from one report to the next).
- **Points by scenario:** VDS, WHO and VIIAS, stating that the years are modelled scenarios.
- **Cards:** single estimates such as the 386 deaths of the expert report.
- **Timeline of cases:** deaths at work.

### Computed annual means (18/11/2026)

`build_air.py` reads the ARPA hourly files of the Taranto stations (`sources/arpa-taranto/`, archive ids in `sources/arpa-archive-ids.txt`). It produces two files:

- `air-data.csv`: all means, each with the number of valid hours.
- `air.js`: only the means with at least 75% valid hours in the year.

**Reading the data:**
- **PM10 at via Machiavelli (Tamburi):** rises from 31.5 µg/m³ (2008) to 36.8 (2011), then falls to 21.4 in 2025.
- **Talsano, the comparison far from the plant:** 24–26 µg/m³ in 2009–2011, about 18–21 since 2017. The gap with Tamburi narrows after 2012.
- **Paolo VI:** falls from about 33 µg/m³ (2005–07, years with few valid hours) to about 16–17 since 2013.

**Known problems:**
- **Machiavelli 2006:** PM10 at 16.4 µg/m³: in every month about half the values of the nearby stations (Archimede 29–57, Talsano 25–42); no ARPA or ISPRA document publishes its mean. Excluded from the site (18/11/2026) as an instrument fault; see `sources/air-extra/air_extra.md`.
- **SO2 and benzene in 2008:** high values at all stations, many zeros at Talsano and anomalous maxima (999 for NO2). These pollutants are not used on the site.
- **Via Orsini (Tamburi):** in the 2024 file the sheet has the header "ILVA 1 ARIA", so it could be a station of the company's network run by ARPA. To be verified before using it.
- **Years without a valid mean at via Machiavelli:** 2005, 2007 and 2013. 2013 is missing from the regional file.
- Benzo(a)pyrene and dioxins are not in the hourly files.

**Positions:** surveyed by hand on the 2023 Regione Puglia orthophoto.
- **Blast furnaces:** 4, recognised from the rows of cowpers, the cast houses and the charging belts. They are 4, like those left after the demolition of AFO3, but **the AFO1/2/4/5 numbering is not assigned** because a site plan is missing.
- **Stack E312:** matches OSM chimney 383684495 within 18 m.
- **Gasholders:** 3, plus 2 cylindrical tanks.
- **Domes:** 3, traced from the photo.

**Dimensions:**
- **Mineral yard cover:** declared 700 × 250–254 m, 77 m high at the extrados (VIA MASE; Cimolai gives 355,600 m²). In the photo its footprint measures about 500 × 270 m per bay: the difference needs to be explained.
- **Everything else is estimated:** E312 210 m (widely cited value, no source found), blast furnaces 85 m, cowpers 50 m, fossil yard 45 m.

**Timing:**
- The map shows the 4 blast furnaces from 1965, but two belong to the 1970–75 doubling: the construction phases are not distinguished.

  - Coke oven stacks: 100–127 m.
  - Cowper stacks: 64–71 m.

### Heights from the laser survey (18/11/2026)

**Data:** PST database of the Ministry of the Environment (sim.mase.gov.it/pst), LiDAR DSM First and DTM at 1 m, licence CC BY 4.0. There are 29 tiles over the plant, from flights in March and December 2008 and in 2010, saved in `sources/lidar/`.
- **Download:** the app's cart sends a POST to `https://sim.mase.gov.it/core/api/esri/api/v1/downloads` with `{canale: 'PST2', prodotto: 'LIDAR_2014', layer: 'dsm_first_1x1' | 'dtm_1x1', tileList: [{fid, tavola, regione, contratto}]}`, at most 20 tiles per request. The status is read at `.../downloads/{idSessione}` and the file at `.../downloads/{id}/file/{n}`. The tiles are listed on `Grigliato_lidar/FeatureServer/2`.
- **Format:** GeoTIFF WGS84, 1000 × 1000 pixels of 1e-5°, nodata -9999.

**Method (`build_heights.py`):** height = DSM − DTM. For each building the 85th percentile of the interior pixels is taken; for stacks, the peak within 15 m.

**Checks against the AIA heights:**

| Stack | LiDAR | AIA |

### Belts, mounds, piles and port (18/11/2026)

- **Elevated belts and pipelines** (`build_conveyors.py` → `conveyors.js`):
  - Method: thin-ridge detector on DSM − DTM at 2 m (cell ≥ 3 m and ground 3 m lower on both sides within 4–6 m, in at least one direction), limited to the plant perimeter.
  - Segments extracted with RANSAC (≥ 40 m, straight); those lying over OSM buildings for more than 30%, i.e. roof ridges, are discarded.
  - Result: 722 segments, about 68 km (ISPRA gives 60 km of belts).
  - The method does not distinguish belts from pipelines: this is why the legend and the card mention them together.
  - Discarded attempts: a 5–8 m threshold with enlarged buildings and an elongation filter, which picked up rows of trees and shed edges.
- **Ecological mounds** (`build_reliefs.py`):
  - Strip traced on the DTM; relief measured as DTM minus the 5th percentile of the ground in 100 m blocks.
  - Result: 9.5 ha, in line with the approx. 9 ha of the 2019 seizure. Shown from 1975 («anni Settanta» [the 1970s]).
- **Yard piles:** the DTM includes them in the ground, so their shape is measured. They cover 5.5 ha in the mineral yard, 6.6 ha in the fossil yard and 2.9 ha in the third yard. They replace the schematic piles.
- **Port** (`build_port.py`):
  - From OSM `osm-port.json`: 5th pier (way 885868986), 5 ArcelorMittal belts with LiDAR elevations, 50 lines of jetties and breakwaters.
  - Cranes: absent from OSM.
